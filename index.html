Live questions in the field

Use these as tags when you post. The science is unsettled. That is the point.

Quantum biology in neurons

Can coherence, tunneling, or nuclear spin survive long enough in warm tissue to change firing, olfaction, or anesthesia?

Orch-OR and microtubules

Penrose–Hameroff still drive experiments on anesthetic binding and claimed room-temperature quantum effects in tubulin.

Quantum-like cognition

Quantum probability as a math language for context, order effects, and “mental entanglement” — even if the wetware is classical.

Quantum sensors & materials

2D semiconductors, NV centers, and nano-NMR as tools to watch cells, not claims that the cell itself is a qubit.

Quantum computing for neuroscience

Using actual quantum hardware or QML on EEG, MRI, connectomes, and protein data that choke classical pipelines.

Neuromorphic quantum hardware

Spin glasses, optical associative memory, and materials that compute like synapses rather than like Turing tapes.

Publisher

Who hosts this desk, and what it is for.

Quantum Cortex Press

This site is hosted by a recent high school student who wants clear writing on quantum physics and neuroscience — not slogans, and not mysticism dressed as a paper.

The aim is simple. Explain the physics carefully. Explain the brain carefully. Say what is measured, what is a model, and what is still a guess. Quantum Cortex is an independent notebook for those notes, not a university press and not a peer-review journal.

About this desk

Built as a working prototype, not a peer-review journal.

Quantum neuroscience sits in a noisy gap. Some papers hunt for real quantum effects inside neurons. Some only borrow quantum math because it fits cognitive data. Some build sensors and algorithms that help neuroscience without claiming the brain is quantum at all. This site keeps those threads in one feed so you can post a preprint, a lab note, or a skeptical comment next to the sample archive.

Honest limit: posts live in localStorage. Open the same browser on this machine and they are still here. Clear site data or switch devices and they are gone. If you want a real multi-user backend later (accounts, uploads, comments), that is the next engineering step.
